§ 02 / The Problem

The people who write the message sit furthest from the buyer.

Your sellers hear the language every day. The objection they have answered forty times. The exact phrase a prospect used right before the deal turned. It is the most honest market research you own, and it disappears the second the call ends.

So the website talks one way and the buyer talks another. The gap is quiet, but it is expensive. It shows up as longer sales cycles, weaker inbound, and a brand that sounds like every other vendor in the category instead of the one that gets it.

§ 03 / The Method

§ 04 / The ProcessFive Weeks

Five weeks, fixed scope, real artifacts at the end.

W1Access

Access & immersion

Kickoff, gather existing sales-call recordings, line up customer interviews, and schedule time with sales, customer success, product, and a founder or exec.

W2–3Field

Field research

Eight to twelve customer interviews, mining of recorded sales calls, and optional time on-site or inside your buyers' channels. Everything captured and structured into the signal library.

W4Synthesis

Synthesis

The patterns surface: the conviction, the verbatim customer language, the recurring objections, and the themes that move conversations forward.

W5Build

Architecture & readout

The messaging architecture gets built and presented, with a clear view of what a content system would put to work next.
